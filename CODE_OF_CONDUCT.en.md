# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

English | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation open and welcoming. Participants should communicate with respect, goodwill, and professionalism regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Unacceptable behavior

This includes personal attacks, harassment, threats, publishing private information, sexualized or discriminatory language, and repeated disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report problems through the private contact configured by the maintainers. Maintainers will investigate fairly and may remove content, issue warnings, or restrict participation.
